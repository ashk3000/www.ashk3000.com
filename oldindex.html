Bio

I'm a high school student at Edgemont Jr./Sr. High School in Westchester, NY. I'm interested in computer science and engineering. At Edgemont I'm president of the Programming Club and a runner on the varsitycross country and track teams.

I've loved programming ever since I started on Scratch and later Roblox Studio. I'm most proficient in Java, but I'm learning C and Rust. Some areas of code that interest me are embedded systems, audio, and graphics. I also have interest in Linux and other open source projects.

When I'm not programming, I like listening to music, practicing piano, and playing video games.

Slide 5

Slide 5 is a board game similar to Connect 4, but there are 2 sides to put pieces in, pieces only move down the board when pushed, and you must get 5 in a row or diagnal to win. My digital recreation of the game includes a terminal and graphical version, along with a bot that you can play against.

2025 Halloween Costume

For Halloween of 2025, I designed and 3D printed a headset for my costume. The headset actually worked, I used the parts from old headphones I had and designed around those to make the parts fit. I used iterative design to continuously tweak the model to get it to work.

Model Train Track Supports

I used OpenSCAD to design supports for a model rail track. OpenSCAD has its own coding language for making 3D Models. This is known as parametric design, and it allows me to easily tweak the model at a later time (primarily so I can change the height of the supports just by changing one number.)
