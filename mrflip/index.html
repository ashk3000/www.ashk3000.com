Info

Mr. Flip is a platformer with a twist. You use your mouse to dash! You can only use your dash once while in the air, so you need to touch the ground to regain it. This game takes inspiration from Celeste by Maddy Makes Games, so if you enjoy Mr. Flip I highly suggest you play Celeste as well.

This game is not compatable with screen readers! It is intended to be played on a pc with a 16:9 1080p monitor.

Controls

Mr. Flip was originally designed to be played with a keyboard and mouse. It also has controller support!

  • W, A, S, D, Arrow Keys, Left Joystick: Move
  • Space, Left Bumper: Jump
  • Move Mouse, Right Joystick: Aim Dash
  • Click, Right Bumper: Dash
  • On controller you must be aiming before you can press the dash button.
